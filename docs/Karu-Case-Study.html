THE CONCEPTA NEW CATEGORY

A gallery for India's makers — selling straight to the world.

For centuries the people who make India's finest crafts sold them for a fraction of their worth. Karu collapses the distance to a single line — maker to collector — and offers two ways to own the work.

OPTION ONE

Acquire a piece

Collect a verified, one-of-one work at a fixed price — or compete for a master piece at live auction. It ships with a certificate of authenticity and the maker's share printed in the open.

OPTION TWO

Commission your own

Have something made only for you — a family deity, a memorial figure. Karu matches you to the right master for the tradition, estimates it, and shepherds it from clay to your door.
