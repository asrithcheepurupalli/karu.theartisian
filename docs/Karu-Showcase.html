Karu · The idea
Who we are01

We are India's artisans — selling direct to the world, for the first time on our own terms.

For generations, the people who make India's finest crafts have sold them for a fraction of their worth. Middlemen took the margin. Buyers across the world never even learned their names — and the traditions quietly died with the generation that held them.

Karu exists to reverse that. A curated house that carries the work of verified masters straight to collectors abroad — preserving the craft, naming the maker, and paying them what they have always deserved. Not an open marketplace. A gallery, with a conscience.

Karu · How it works
The model05

A curated house — where buying is an experience, and the maker is the point.

Curated marketplace

Selected, not listed

Every artisan is hand-picked and every piece meets a single standard — would this belong in a serious collection? This protects quality, trust, and premium positioning.

Revenue

70–80% to the maker

The platform takes only what keeps the house running. The split is transparent on every product page, so buyers always know exactly whose work their money supports.

Live auctions

Collectors set the value

Master works are released to live auction with a maker-set reserve. Because the artisan's share is fixed, every bid increases what they take home.

Commissions

Made only for you

Collectors can request bespoke work — a family deity, a memorial piece — matched to the right master, estimated, created, documented and shipped.
